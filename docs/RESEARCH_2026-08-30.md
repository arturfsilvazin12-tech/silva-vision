# Silva Vision — Deep Research Log 2026-08-30

## Research scope

Research cross-checked public GitHub projects, ReShade shader repositories, FiveM/Cfx.re discussions, GTA V modding discussions and Reddit reports. The goal is not to copy third-party packs, but to identify reproducible engineering ideas and compatibility hazards.

## Findings adopted

### A. Separate engine-side and post-process responsibilities
`visualsettings.dat` and timecycle/weather data can influence lighting, weather, sky, vehicle lighting and atmospheric behavior. ReShade should therefore be used primarily for controlled post-processing instead of compensating for every world-level problem.

### B. Live iteration is valuable
Public tooling such as fxReloader demonstrates a development workflow where visualsettings and timecycle files can be reloaded without a full game restart. This is treated as a development reference, not a dependency of the release package.

### C. Depth is conditional
AO, SSR, GI, DOF and other depth-dependent effects require a valid depth buffer. Community reports show AA/upscalers can interfere with depth access. Silva Vision therefore needs a depth-health gate and safe fallback profiles.

### D. Shader order matters
Public shader repositories document suggested ordering and show that color effects can produce materially different results depending on when they run. Silva Vision will use an explicit pipeline rather than enabling effects alphabetically.

### E. Debanding is a quality tool, not decoration
Debanding can improve smooth sky/atmospheric gradients and can include depth-aware behavior. It belongs late in the color pipeline, after major grading changes but before final display sharpening where appropriate.

### F. Performance must be measured per effect
Community reports show ReShade GPU time can jump substantially when an effect or effect combination misbehaves. Profiles therefore need a cost class and a safe fallback instead of a single 'everything on' preset.

### G. Water and weather need independent ownership
Water, rain, timecycle and visualsettings can overlap. A conflict matrix is required so two layers do not simultaneously fight for the same visual responsibility.

### H. Avoid artificial global fog removal
Removing all fog can increase apparent distance but can damage atmospheric depth. Silva Vision will use controlled haze/fog as a depth cue and only reduce it where the weather profile calls for it.

### I. Do not solve LOD problems with excessive sharpening
Community reports of pop-in/flicker show that streaming/LOD and shadow/reflection behavior can be separate bottlenecks. Sharpening will not be used as a substitute for world-data fixes.

## Sources worth keeping

- crosire/reshade-shaders — canonical public ReShade shader collection.
- prod80/prod80-ReShade-Repository — detailed color/contrast/LUT/depth-aware post-processing.
- martymcmodding/iMMERSE — modern ReShade effects and technical documentation.
- CeeJayDK/SweetFX — SMAA, CAS, curves, tonemap and established post-processing techniques.
- crxhvrd/fxReloader — live visualsettings/timecycle iteration reference.
- public FiveM visualsettings repositories — useful parameter inventory, not authoritative values.

## Non-adoption rules

- Do not copy proprietary NVE/QuantV/ENB assets.
- Do not redistribute third-party shaders without checking license compatibility.
- Do not blindly combine graphics packs.
- Do not claim an untested value is optimal.
- Do not rename native files merely for organization.
